How to play

Start with one ball. Each bounce adds 1 damage, at 15 it becomes unstable. Kill enemies to delay overload. Catch it to throw it again.

  • +1Each bounce off a wall or obstacle adds 1 damage to your ball. Enemy health is written at its center. Enemies with no number have 1 health.
  • ↶Hold pull to bend the ball toward you.
  • ×Chain kills to increase your score multiplier. The higher the charge, the higher the score.
    Chain 10 kills to shield your ship for 5 seconds.
  • ◇Pull the ball until it reaches your ship to catch it. Catching it with 10 or more charges blasts enemies around you. The higher the charge, the bigger the blast.
  • ⁂Bosses grant rewards: a wingman or extra Hull.
    Wingman: a smaller ball that follows every throw. You can bend it, but you can’t catch it. It comes home when you catch your main ball. It only overloads when your main ball overloads.
    Hull: your ship’s health.
  • ◆Enemies may drop Capsules. Capsules are marked with different icons, each granting a unique power-up. Hit a Capsule with your ball to collect it. The more charge the ball has, the longer and/or stronger the power-up.
  • ♥Score milestones repair one Hull point, up to your Hull's cap (3 to start). The next milestone is displayed under your Hull.
  • 15At 15 bounces, your ball enters overload. After 2 seconds, the next bounce causes it to explode, killing enemies around the blast and resetting your multiplier. Each kill resets the 2-second timer. After overloading, the ball is out of play until you pick it up.
Keyboard · WASD or arrows to fly. Mouse click or Space: press to throw, hold to pull
Mobile · one thumb anywhere on the left half flies, the other on the right half holds to pull and swipes to throw. Landscape orientation is strongly recommended.
